# Green Way Transportation LLC

Five-page static website: Home, Services, About, Contact, and Request a Quote. Files and assets are at the repository root; no build step is needed.

The right way is the green way.

Green Way is based in Phoenix and offers transportation across the United States. The site uses the supplied official logo and all ten company photographs, with responsive WebP sizes and lazy loading for gallery photos. Photo captions are omitted; descriptive alt text and accessible photo links are retained. The original homepage photo is displayed in a larger, full-width layout up to 1600 pixels without cropping the load.

The two-second truck intro covers loading of styles, fonts, logos, and eager images. It appears once per tab session when storage is available. Reduced-motion and JavaScript-disabled visitors see the page directly. Escape dismisses it; failed images and loading deadlines are handled safely.

Quote labels, optional notes, and paired inputs align even when a title wraps. Forms stack on smaller screens, mobile menus scroll on short screens, and footer links have larger touch targets.

Forms validate details and prepare an email draft to Operations.greenwaycarrier@gmail.com. Visitors must send it from their email app; this site does not automatically submit or store requests.

Validation: local assets, form associations, JavaScript syntax, intro behavior, and menu/form interactions checked. Browser rendering and measured performance still need final review.
